Extending Banica–Özteke–Pittau's Fourier defect theory: a correction to their truncation conjecture

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

Extending Banica–Özteke–Pittau's Fourier defect theory, we refute their Conjecture 1.14 (Isolated partial Hadamard matrices, and related topics, arXiv:1706.00986v2, p. 9): Fourier incidence equations and the analytic implicit-function theorem give non-isolation after any two row deletions at every prime order p ≥ 5. For any nonempty proper row set of size m, the local dephased solution space is a real-analytic manifold of dimension (m−1)(p−m−1). Thus among the nonempty prime Fourier truncations, precisely those with m = 1, p−1, p are isolated, and no fixed positive density margin ensures isolation. We also give an explicit defect formula for every row truncation of a finite abelian Fourier matrix; its zero-cycle case has the same analytic dimension formula with p replaced by the group order.
